# ADLIB-93 listening acceptance

Status: **PENDING**. No claim is made that these presets were heard, accepted,
level matched or commercially tuned. Synthetic fixtures verify engineering
behavior only. No existing recording has been imported, changed or published.

Use only owner-cleared dry ad-lib performances: spoken, whispered, shouted,
sibilant and sung. For each exact factory preset test mono/stereo and solo/in
mix. `audition-matrix.csv` contains all 480 combinations, initially PENDING.
Record source provenance/permission, sample rate, bit depth, channels, file
hash, preset revision, binary hash, comparison trims and listener/date.

Compare active-phrase RMS within 0.5 dB of bypass and a simple reference chain.
Exclude silence/tails from the active-phrase measurement, retain original tails
in playback/export, and record the measurement window explicitly. Do not
normalize away intentional echoes or infer subjective transparency from meters.

Listen for preset identity/usefulness, level jumps, clicks during automation,
gate consonant loss, de-ess lisping, pitch/comb changes on mono fold-down,
distortion aliasing (particularly hard/asymmetric at44.1kHz), Freeze overlap,
Throw release and tail truncation. Record exact reproduction steps and audible
time locations. Preset trims/levels change only after demonstrated issues with
before/after values, reasons and affected automated tests rerun.

Do not claim an acoustic audition from the mere existence of generated WAVs,
waveform plots or successful validators. Owner acceptance remains required for
musical identity and quality. Distribution and demonstration publishing remain
separate owner decisions.
