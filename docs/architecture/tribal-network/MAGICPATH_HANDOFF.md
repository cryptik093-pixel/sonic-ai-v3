# MagicPath Handoff — Tribal Network™

## Purpose

Use the documents in this folder as the authoritative product-design package for Tribal Network. Do not reduce the concept to a social profile, portfolio page, public EPK, or generic project-management dashboard.

## Product thesis

Tribal Network is the private creative-intelligence network of The Tribe.

It should continuously organize and relate the work produced across the platform's creative stations so a member can visually understand:
- what they are creating;
- what projects and releases are evolving;
- what techniques, assets, and evidence are accumulating;
- what collaborations and services they can access or contribute;
- what patterns Sonic Intelligence detects across their career;
- how their identity and body of work are changing through repeated creative cycles.

The member should be able to work freely while the underlying system remembers structurally.

## Interface hierarchy

The dedicated dashboard is the operating environment: **What should I do now?**

Tribal Network is the living identity / career / project intelligence station: **What am I building, becoming, contributing, and connecting to?**

The profile is not primarily a public destination. It is an internal visual mirror and intelligence surface for:
- the creator themself;
- approved collaborators;
- Tribe members;
- stewardship / intelligence functions according to permissions.

## Required design qualities

The experience should feel:
- enormous;
- open;
- calm;
- premium;
- spatial;
- compact without crowding;
- information-dense without visual noise;
- private and intentional;
- professional enough for elite creator operations;
- expressive enough to preserve individual creative identity.

Maintain the white / gunmetal visual language unless a later explicit product decision supersedes it.

## Architectural principle

Other stations generate work and evidence. Tribal Network organizes their consequences.

Examples:
- Creation Station -> experiments, drafts, sessions.
- Project / Release Station -> structured project and release objects.
- Collaboration -> contributors, roles, deliverables, credits.
- Practice / Learning -> developmental evidence and mastered capabilities.
- Asset Station -> stems, presets, reusable techniques, references, lineage.
- Opportunity / Services -> availability, offers, internal Tribe needs.
- Tribal Network -> one connected visual career model.
- Sonic Intelligence -> underlying memory, relationships, pattern recognition, recommendations, continuity.

## Initiation principle

Treat initiation as a **soft system principle**, not a heavy front-facing doctrine.

A first entrance may be linear, but development is cyclical:
`WORK -> EXPERIENCE -> EVIDENCE -> REFLECTION -> RECOGNITION -> NEXT DIRECTION -> WORK`

The UI can express this softly through Current Focus, Season, Practice, Evidence, Reflection, Transition, and Next Threshold rather than forcing ceremonial language everywhere.

## Non-negotiable product rules

1. Do not design follower counts, likes, or public popularity as the primary reputation mechanism.
2. Reputation should emerge from practice, evidence, contribution, collaboration, completion, and recognized capability.
3. Do not make constant manual documentation a burden; the platform should capture structure from real work whenever possible.
4. Keep public export optional and separate from the private canonical profile.
5. Preserve experimentation, abandoned work, and failure as useful private intelligence when the member chooses to retain it.
6. Sonic Intelligence should strengthen distinct creative identity, not homogenize creators.
7. Every screen must be judged against the North-Star documents, not merely against the current prototype.

## First design target

Design the Tribal Network home/profile station inside the persistent Tribe dashboard shell.

The first screen should make the member's evolving career legible at a glance:
- identity;
- current focus;
- featured / active work;
- active projects and releases;
- daily / seasonal creative activity;
- collaborations;
- services and availability;
- developmental evidence;
- meaningful metrics;
- Sonic Intelligence observations / next-best actions;
- lineage / history access.
