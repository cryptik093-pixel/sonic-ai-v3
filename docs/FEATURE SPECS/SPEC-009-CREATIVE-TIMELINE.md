# SPEC-009 — CREATIVE TIMELINE

Version 1.0

Status: Approved

Classification: Core Experience Engine

Priority: Critical

---

# Purpose

The Creative Timeline is the chronological narrative of a creator's life inside Sonic AI V3.

Rather than organizing work into disconnected folders, Sonic organizes creative growth through time.

Every session, decision, milestone, breakthrough, release, and lesson becomes part of a continuous story.

The Timeline transforms isolated projects into a lifelong creative journey.

---

# Core Philosophy

Folders organize files.

Timelines organize lives.

Every meaningful creative event deserves context.

The Timeline exists to preserve the story behind the work—not just the work itself.

---

# Mission

The Creative Timeline answers one essential question:

**"How did we get here?"**

It records not only what happened, but what changed, what was learned, and how those moments influenced everything that followed.

---

# Timeline Events

The Timeline captures meaningful moments such as:

- Projects Created
- Sessions Started
- Songs Completed
- Voice Notes
- Ideas Captured
- Creative Breakthroughs
- Knowledge Gained
- Studio DNA Evolution
- Client Milestones
- Collaborations
- Releases
- Awards
- Failures
- Recoveries
- Major Decisions
- Workflow Changes
- Career Milestones

Every event contributes to the larger narrative of the creator.

---

# Event Context

Each Timeline event should answer:

- When did it happen?
- Why did it matter?
- What changed?
- Who was involved?
- What did we learn?
- What happened next?

Context is more valuable than timestamps.

---

# AI Responsibilities

The AI acts as a creative historian.

It should:

- Surface forgotten breakthroughs.
- Reconnect related moments.
- Explain long-term growth.
- Celebrate meaningful achievements.
- Suggest reflection when appropriate.
- Preserve perspective without overwhelming the creator.

The Creative Timeline exists so those moments are never lost.
