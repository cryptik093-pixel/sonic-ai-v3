## 1. PURPOSE

This insert establishes a formal named-studio collaboration layer for selected recipients of the Studio Drop.002 production assets.

The collaboration model allows Omega House Studio to provide the designated production assets at no charge to an approved studio while preserving product identity, provenance, attribution, license boundaries, and controlled use.

This insert is intended to accompany the applicable production package. It is **not a replacement for the governing license agreement**. Where this insert conflicts with an executed license or written agreement, the governing license/agreement controls.

---

# 2. LICENSE CLASS

### BASIC — STUDIO COLLABORATIVE

This is a **basic-use license granted on a studio-specific basis**.

The recipient receives authorized access to the identified Studio Drop.002 production assets for permitted music-production and creative-development use within the named studio environment.

The studio-specific designation means the authorization is granted to the named recipient/studio and is not automatically transferable to another studio, company, person, label, client, or organization.

**No separate monetary purchase is required for the specifically authorized collaboration delivery unless an executed agreement states otherwise.**

Free delivery does not mean unrestricted ownership or unrestricted redistribution.

---

# 3. AUTHORIZED COLLABORATIVE USE

Unless the governing license states otherwise, authorized uses may include:

- music production;
- songwriting and composition;
- beat production;
- arrangement and reconstruction;
- editing and resampling;
- layering and sound design;
- recording and production sessions;
- development of original musical works;
- internal studio experimentation and demonstration;
- use by authorized studio personnel working within the approved collaboration.

The recipient may incorporate the supplied production assets into original musical works subject to the governing license terms.

---

# 4. RESTRICTIONS

The Studio Collaborative license does **not** by itself authorize:

- resale of the raw or substantially unaltered assets;
- redistribution of the production package as a competing sample/melody/stem product;
- sublicensing or assignment to an unrelated third party;
- claiming authorship or ownership of Omega House source assets themselves;
- removal or alteration of provenance, certification, or product-identification records where those records accompany the licensed package;
- use outside the authorized recipient/studio scope where the governing license restricts such use;
- representation that the recipient owns the underlying Omega House source assets outright.

Commercial exploitation, client delivery, label delivery, synchronization, exclusivity, work-for-hire treatment, ownership transfer, or other rights beyond the basic collaborative scope require the applicable written authorization.
