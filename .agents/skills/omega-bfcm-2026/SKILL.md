# Omega House Beats — Black Friday 2026

Use [references/campaign-playbook.md](references/campaign-playbook.md) as the **approved campaign brief**, not proof of current storefront state. Interpret it alongside the repository's actual README, AGENTS.md, code, test results, and connected Shopify records. Keep Omega House OS (operating knowledge), Sonic AI V3 (evidence/production tooling), Omega House Beats (commerce), and Tribal Network (creator community) distinct.

## Mission and non-negotiables

Deliver measurable, profitable purchases and owned-customer growth under the positioning **“Original sound. Clear rights. From first idea to finished record.”** Do not imitate large sample marketplaces by emphasizing inflated file counts. Keep the $5 Producer Starter as the permanent entry; treat the $39 Session Kit and $79 Producer Vault as proposed campaign SKUs requiring validation before publishing. Do not purchase apps, start paid ads, send external marketing, alter live prices, publish products, or deploy a store without explicit authorization and working credentials. Preserve existing content, customer records, sessions, licensing, source assets, and approved artifacts.

## Execution protocol

1. **Discover evidence.** Identify repository, branch, active theme, store connection and current product/variant IDs; read current catalogs, prices, licenses, digital files, inventory/capacity, checkout and events. Mark every finding as verified, reported, proposed, or blocked. Never pretend a connector is active.
2. **Resolve offers.** Confirm bundle eligibility, rights compatibility, downloadable-file size/access and independent current prices. Recompute savings from actual current figures; never fabricate compare-at history. Protect labor margins and exclusives. Founder cap is real only if the allocator enforces 500.
3. **Build conversion flow.** Mobile-first audio hero → producer/artist choice → $5/$39/$79 product comparison (artist $99 service lane) → actual inclusions → demo → license summary → FAQ/trust → sticky purchase CTA → checkout → download/access → relevant post-purchase offer. Follow store's native theme conventions; avoid heavy scripts.
4. **Instrument before optimization.** Capture first-party campaign/UTM identity and standard Shopify/GA4 commerce events; use custom events only where useful. Deduplicate client/server purchase events, enforce consent, minimize PII and report data gaps.
5. **Operate the calendar.** Oct 20–31 inventory/design/QA; Nov 1–15 organic discovery and consented list capture; Nov 16–25 segmented warmup; Nov 26–30 limited-time live campaign; from Dec 1 delivery, activation, support, retention and measurement.
6. **Validate end to end.** Check phone viewport, audio keyboard controls, page performance, price math, two audience routes, discount behavior, licenses, Shop Pay/guest checkout availability, digital delivery, real capacity limits, email consent, order attribution, refund/support path and expiration in America/Chicago.
7. **Close with evidence.** Report changed files/objects, commands/tests, screenshots or links, measured baseline and post-change conversion, remaining blockers, and exact activation status. Distinguish drafts from deployed changes.

## Scoring and decisions

North star: **net revenue/contribution**, with gross revenue as the initial $500 milestone. Secondary: qualified sessions, product-page → add-to-cart, checkout completion, revenue/session, average order value, sample-play rate, email capture with consent, attach rate, refund rate and customer activation. Compare producers and artists separately. Prefer a falsifiable experiment and simple offers over complicated coupon stacks. Do not make forecasted uplift claims without data.

## Deliverable standard

For an implementation request, actually edit files and tests when connected and permitted; do not stop at recommendations. Prefer native Shopify capabilities and existing repository components, with no new paid dependencies. For a planning request, produce concrete specs, copy, data definitions and release gates. Treat the reference's competitor statistics/2026 forecasts and their citations as **user-supplied research requiring fresh source verification before external publication**.
