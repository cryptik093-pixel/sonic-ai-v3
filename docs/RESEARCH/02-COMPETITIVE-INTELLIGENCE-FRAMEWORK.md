Executive Summary

Competitive intelligence is not the process of copying successful companies.

It is the disciplined process of understanding:

Why companies succeed.
Why users love them.
Why users leave them.
What they intentionally ignore.
What market they truly own.
What opportunities remain unclaimed.

Every competitor studied by Sonic AI will be analyzed objectively.

The purpose is not imitation.

The purpose is strategic understanding.

Mission

Develop the industry's most comprehensive understanding of the music production software ecosystem.

Every research document should improve Sonic AI's ability to make better product decisions.

Guiding Principles

Competitive research exists to answer one question:

How can Sonic AI create a category that currently does not exist?

Never ask:

"How can we build what they built?"

Always ask:

"Why did they build it this way?"

Closing Principle

We are not studying competitors to become them. We are studying competitors to understand the landscape they created, so we can build something that redefines it.

Architect's Notes

This document establishes a habit that I want us to keep throughout the life of Sonic AI V3: every opinion should eventually be supported by evidence. When we later decide, for example, that the Project Wizard should have six steps instead of three, or that AI should proactively surface saved vocal chains, we'll be able to point back to research, user behavior, and our design principles—not just intuition.

As our documentation grows, we'll build a chain of reasoning:

Research explains the market.
Design explains our philosophy.
Architecture explains the system.
Engineering explains the implementation.
Decision Logs explain why we chose one path over another.

That chain is what turns a collection of features into a coherent product vision.
