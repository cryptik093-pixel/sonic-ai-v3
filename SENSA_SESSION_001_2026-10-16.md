## Craft decisions
Central tension: chosen work can be fulfilling while consuming irreplaceable time.
Metaphor mechanism: repeated guitar playing physically wears skin and strings; repeated practice also spends finite life. A recording preserves an expression of a night, not the night itself.
Sound chain: thin / skin / again (last is a slant dependent on pronunciation); slipped / grip / gives in uses short-i assonance and consonant movement, not a claimed exact multisyllabic rhyme.
Hook initially admits uncertainty; verse can supply concrete cost; returning hook can carry both ownership and loss.
Hypothetical stress anchors: WASTE / LIFE; GUESS / LIVE(D) / LIFE / WANT. No measured beat placement, BPM, key, meter, pitch contour or bar fit has been established.
