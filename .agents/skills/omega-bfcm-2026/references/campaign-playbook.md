## Strategy

**Brand promise:** “Original sound. Clear rights. From first idea to finished record.”

**Differentiation:** Do not try to beat Splice on millions of samples, Cymatics on steep perpetual promotions, ProducerGrind on mass free-library/community volume, or Unison on exhaustive long-form direct-response copy. Win with original cinematic hip-hop/trap/soul sound, coherent production systems, high-proof demonstrations, transparent licensing, human producer identity and the bridge from assets to a finished release. No fake social proof, fake inventory caps, exaggerated hit-making claims, or arbitrary markdowns.

**Audience 1: producers:** Need distinctive usable sound, stems, drums, MIDI, presets, clear royalty/licensing terms and session-ready grouping. **Audience 2: artists:** Need audibly differentiated beats, rights at a glance, clear deliverables and optionally custom production/mixing/mastering. **Lifecycle:** discover audio → $5 entry → deeper system → beat license → song completion → repeat purchase and referrals.

## Funnel and site architecture

**Acquisition:** Organic short-form demonstration, producer breakdowns, YouTube, consented email, SEO, partner creator sample exchange, free preview/sample with valid rights, and referral; do not enable paid Meta/TikTok/YouTube placements without budget approval. Attach UTM source/medium/campaign/content. Campaign identifier `bfcm_2026`.

**Landing page, in order:**
1. Slim announcement strip: Black Friday + genuine deadline + “Original sound. Clear rights.”
2. Mobile-first hero: “BUILD A RECORD THAT DOESN'T SOUND LIKE EVERYBODY ELSE'S.” Short copy: cinematic beats, MIDI, drums, stems, production systems; instant delivery for eligible digital products. Two controls: **Hear the Vault** and **Get the $79 Vault**.
3. Three real playable audio previews with verified titles, BPM and key when known. No guessed metadata.
4. **Choose your path:** “I'm a Producer” vs “I'm an Artist” (remember selection for analytics, not intrusive profiling).
5. Producer good/better/best: **$5 Starter**, **$39 Session Kit**, **$79 Vault**; separate artist beat-license and **$99 Complete Song** lane. One clearly featured offer, not three competing primary CTAs.
6. Contents and format chart: each actual product, stems/MIDI/WAV/presets, versions, file count, FL Studio/VST compatibility where tested; clickable detail.
7. Real session proof: raw sound → arrangement → final mix short A/B; authentic waveform/audio, screenshots, demonstration and credits.
8. Compact license explainer: what can be released, what files are delivered, streaming/monetization rights where contract permits, prohibited redistribution, upgrade path; link full product-specific license.
9. Trust and objections: who created it, accurate delivery expectations, real testimonials only, plugin support/installation, refund policy, support contact, license FAQs.
10. Repeat CTA + honest deadline; sticky mobile CTA for active offer; continue smoothly to Shopify checkout.
11. Post-purchase: automatic authorized digital fulfillment, onboarding/use guide, context-relevant artist/producer cross-sell, later email follow-up if consented.

**UX and performance:** One shared accessible audio player; lazy-load below-fold media, compress images, defer nonessential scripts, no unnecessary video embeds above fold, no layout jumps from previews. Target Core Web Vitals guidelines (LCP ≤2.5s; INP ≤200ms; CLS ≤0.1) measured on real mobile field data where available, not assumed. Provide keyboard/pause/volume controls, descriptive buttons, readable small-phone price/licensing content. Guest checkout and eligible accelerated checkout must not be blocked by account creation.

**Shopify technical policy:** Native-first catalog/products/variants, sections, collections, discounts, checkout, Digital Products delivery and first-party customer events. Verify app pricing, current availability and per-file limits against actual admin before making claims. Use storefront or Admin APIs only with correct authorized scopes and current version. No paid app dependencies by default.

## Measurement contract

Standard commerce events where available: `view_item_list`, `select_item`, `view_item`, `add_to_cart`, `begin_checkout`, `purchase`, `refund`. Campaign-specific interaction events: `bfcm_audio_play`, `bfcm_audience_selected`, `bfcm_license_open`, `bfcm_offer_compare`, `bfcm_download_access`. Include event_id, campaign_id, page/placement, anonymous session and product/variant identifiers, value/currency where applicable, consent state; never put email, full names or raw access tokens in marketing analytics. Deduplicate purchase measurement and verify event counts against real Shopify paid orders. Treat consented opt-in capture separately from checkout email.

**Daily scorecard** by producer/artist, source and landing variant: qualified sessions; audio-play rate; view → cart; cart → checkout; checkout → paid purchase; conversion rate; AOV; gross/net revenue; revenue/session; orders by offer; attach rate; refunds/chargebacks; email opt-in; support incidence; mobile page vitals. Evaluate contribution and cost of labor before increasing incentives. Sample sizes and uncertainty must be explicit. Do not present made-up lift percentages.

**Simple tests:** (A) audio hero vs static display, (B) producer-first vs audience-choice opening, (C) Vault default vs Kit default, (D) outcome-oriented vs file-count copy; change one material factor per test when traffic is sparse. Lower traffic may justify qualitative session/record review over unsupported significance claims.
