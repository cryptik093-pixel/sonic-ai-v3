Founder Reflection

This document marks an important transition in Sonic AI V3. Up to now, we've been discussing architecture and features. This strategy defines why the architecture exists.

As we've talked over the past several days, one theme has consistently emerged: we're not trying to win by adding more features than everyone else. We're trying to create a product that feels fundamentally different to use.

I want to add one final principle that I believe should guide us for years:

The 10x Rule of Product Design

When evaluating any major feature, don't ask:

"Is this better?"

Ask:

"Is this an order of magnitude better?"

If another platform saves a producer five minutes, can Sonic save thirty?

If another platform remembers filenames, can Sonic remember creative decisions?

If another platform organizes samples, can Sonic organize an entire creative career?

That mindset keeps us from chasing parity. It pushes us toward building experiences that feel genuinely new.
