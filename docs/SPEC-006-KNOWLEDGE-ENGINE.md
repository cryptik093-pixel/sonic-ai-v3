# SPEC-006 — KNOWLEDGE ENGINE

Status: Approved for Version 1 Design

Classification: Core Intelligence System

Priority: Critical

---

# Purpose

The Knowledge Engine transforms creative experience into reusable understanding.

It exists to answer one question:

"What have we learned?"

Not what happened.

Not what was clicked.

Not what was saved.

What was learned.

The Knowledge Engine converts years of creative work into practical intelligence that improves every future project.

Knowledge compounds.

That compounding is one of the defining characteristics of Sonic AI V3.

---

# Core Philosophy

Memory preserves experience.

Knowledge explains experience.

Memory remembers.

Knowledge understands.

The purpose of the Knowledge Engine is not to collect information.

Its purpose is to continuously build a richer understanding of how creativity actually works inside a studio.

---

# Mission

The Knowledge Engine continuously identifies patterns that repeatedly create value.

It asks:

What solved the problem?

Why did it work?

When does it work?

Where does it work?

How confident are we?

Can this help future work?

If the answer is yes, the lesson becomes Knowledge.

---

# Definition of Knowledge

Knowledge is verified understanding created from repeated evidence.

Knowledge is never assumed.

Knowledge is never guessed.

Knowledge is earned.

# Retrieval

Knowledge appears only when useful.

Examples:

Starting a similar project

Working with a familiar artist

Mixing a recurring genre

Preparing mastering

Exporting for streaming

Client review

Knowledge should reduce friction.

Never create it.

---

# AI Responsibilities

The AI explains.

The AI teaches.

The AI recalls.

The AI connects.

The AI never invents.

When presenting knowledge the AI should always answer:

Why is this relevant?

Where was this learned?

How confident is this?

What evidence supports it?
