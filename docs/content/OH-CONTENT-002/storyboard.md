## Avatar performance direction

Play the ambassador as a seasoned producer who has already heard this failure many times: still posture, economical hand gestures, steady eye line, slight head tilt when diagnosing, restrained smile only after the listener recognizes the problem. Use clean articulation and purposeful pauses. No exaggerated wolf behavior, snarling, cartoon reaction, lip-sync drift, extra limbs, wardrobe changes, added markings, or identity redesign.
