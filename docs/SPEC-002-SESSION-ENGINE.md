# SPEC-002 — SESSION ENGINE

Status: Approved for Version 1 Design

Owner: Sonic AI V3

---

# Purpose

The Session Engine is the heartbeat of Sonic AI.

Projects are permanent.

Sessions are alive.

Every interaction inside Sonic AI happens within a session.

Rather than tracking only files, Sonic AI captures the creative process itself.

A session represents one continuous period of creative work and becomes the smallest meaningful unit of creation inside the Producer Operating System.

---

# Philosophy

Traditional software remembers what was created.

Sonic remembers how it was created.

Every session contributes to the long-term intelligence of:

• The Project

• The Studio

• The Producer

• Studio DNA

• Legacy Intelligence

The goal is not surveillance.

The goal is preserving creative experience.

---

# Definition

A Session begins when a creator intentionally enters a Project Workspace.

A Session ends when creative work stops.

Sessions can span minutes or many hours.

A project contains many sessions.

A career contains thousands of sessions.

# Relationship to Other Systems

Projects contain Sessions.

---

# Engineering Principles

Sessions are immutable historical records.

No information is discarded.

Every meaningful action is timestamped.

Every note belongs somewhere.

Every decision has context.

Sessions must remain lightweight, searchable, and expandable.
