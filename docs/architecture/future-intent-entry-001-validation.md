# Future Intent Entry 001 — Validation Protocol

## Objective

Turn the Omega House legacy expansion intent into a longitudinal test of Sonic's ability to track goals, portfolio momentum, obstacles, interventions, timing, creator intuition, foresight, and outcomes.

## Launch decision vector

```text
L = {R, P, T, C, E}
```

Where:

- `R` = revenue activation evidence
- `P` = flagship portfolio balance evidence
- `T` = timing evidence
- `C` = creator creative-readiness evidence
- `E` = evidence quality

Release recommendation:

```text
READY_FOR_DECISION iff R ∧ P ∧ T ∧ C ∧ E
```

This is a decision-support gate. It does not imply automatic release.

## Revenue test

Observe a configured time window and record net revenue, gross revenue, orders, refunds/reversals, trend, and observation duration.

Target operating band: `$4,000–$10,000/month`.

A single spike is insufficient evidence of sustained activation.

## Portfolio test

Track **Heirglyphic** and **Rebellion** independently.

Do not require equal sales. Establish a healthy range from observed traffic, product age, pricing, exposure, and sales distribution. Measure whether the mix remains within that range and diagnose causes when it does not.

## Intervention test

When an imbalance or blocker is detected:

```text
baseline → intervention → observation window → result → delta
```

The system stores both successful and unsuccessful interventions.

## Timing test

Timing is evaluated from current trajectory and contextual evidence. Sonic records the rationale and evidence used rather than emitting an unexplained score.

## Intuition test

The creator explicitly records readiness as `ready`, `not_ready`, or `uncertain`, with rationale. Sonic may compare creator intuition with observed evidence but cannot impersonate the creator's intuition.

## Post-launch validation

If the product launches, compare forecasts with actual results at 7-day and 30-day checkpoints where data exists.

Measure forecast error and calibration over repeated intents rather than declaring success from one prediction.

## Vol. Two hypothesis

**Omega House Beats Premier PREMIUM Vol. Two — Cinematic Hard-Hitting Samples & Loop Kit** is the proposed next legacy chapter. A six-pack decomposition remains a future product architecture hypothesis until validated.

## Success of the intelligence system

The test succeeds only if Sonic can reconstruct the chain:

```text
intent
→ gates
→ evidence
→ diagnosis
→ intervention
→ decision
→ launch
→ outcome
→ forecast error
→ learned evidence
```

The purpose is to improve the *decision process*, not merely to predict a sale.
