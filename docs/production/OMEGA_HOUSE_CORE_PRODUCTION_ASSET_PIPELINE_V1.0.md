## 1. System Purpose

The Omega House Core Production Asset Pipeline is a modular production system designed to turn one controlled creative session into multiple commercially useful, technically traceable assets.

The system begins with **fully engineered melodic one-shots and their source presets**. Those sounds become the foundation for a **16-bar melody**. The melody feeds the creation of **stems, MIDI, and the final composition**.

The finished production is then mastered, its engineering decisions are preserved, and the resulting material is packaged into four standalone products plus one premium bundle.

### Core principle

**Create once → derive many times → preserve everything → monetize at multiple levels.**

           MIDI        COMPOSITION
            ↓               ↓
      MIDI LOOP KIT      BEAT PACK
              \           /

The Sound Kit is the foundational asset layer and contains the source sounds, associated presets, and preserved master/engineering chains.

## 3. The Five Commercial Assets

| Asset | Primary Contents | Commercial Role |
|---|---|---|
| **01 — Sound Kit** | Engineered one-shots, source presets, production presets, master chains | Sound-design + engineering product |
| **02 — Melody + Stems Pack** | 16-bar melody and individual musical stems | Reconstruction/remixing product |
| **03 — MIDI Loop Kit** | Cleaned MIDI derived from the musical source | Composition/data product |
| **04 — Beat Pack** | Finished/mastered compositions | Finished-production product |
| **05 — Production Suite** | Assets 01–04 | Premium ecosystem/bundle |

**Important:** The Production Suite is not independently manufactured. It is the aggregation layer for the four underlying products.

Every production receives a unique Production ID.

Example: `OH-RBLV1-001`

This establishes permanent **asset provenance**.

## 5. Stage 01 — Engineered One-Shot Foundation

Build the controlled melodic sound vocabulary before composition begins.

Typical categories:

- Piano
- Bass
- Synth
- Pluck
- Bell
- Texture
- Pad
- Other melodic instruments

Each one-shot must be intentionally designed, cleanly edited, properly gain-staged, tuned where applicable, processed appropriately, rendered, QC checked, named consistently, cataloged, and paired with its source preset when applicable.

A sound does not enter the production library merely because it sounds interesting. It becomes an approved asset only after it passes the engineering gate.

---

## 6. One-Shot / Preset Pairing Rule

When a one-shot originates from a synthesizer, sampler, or instrument, the source preset remains permanently associated with the rendered audio.

```text
OH-RBLV1-S01/
├── Audio/
│   └── OH-RBLV1-S01.wav
├── Preset/
│   └── OH-RBLV1-S01.fst
├── Preview/
│   └── OH-RBLV1-S01.mp3
└── Metadata/
    └── OH-RBLV1-S01.json
```

**Rule:** The preset is part of the asset, not an optional attachment.

Where practical, loading the preset should reproduce the source sound used to create the rendered one-shot.

## 7. Stage 02 — 16-Bar Melody

The engineered one-shots become the musical vocabulary for the production.

The next production unit is the **16-bar melody**.

The melody establishes:

- Key
- BPM
- Scale
- Harmonic identity
- Main motif
- Sonic character
- Instrumentation
- Musical identity
- Omega House character

### Melody approval gate

The melody advances only when it demonstrates:

- Musical quality
- Memorability
- Sonic identity
- Production usability
- Extractability
- Product potential

**The melody is the master creative seed.**

---

## 8. Stage 03 — Stems

The approved melody is separated into its musical components.

Possible stem categories:

- Piano
- Bass
- Synth
- Pluck
- Bell
- Texture
- Pad
- Other melodic elements

### Source-of-truth rule

Stems must originate from the actual production session. Do not create replacement versions solely for packaging when the production already contains the correct source.

---

## 9. Stage 04 — MIDI

MIDI is derived from the same musical performance/source that produced the audio.

Possible MIDI assets:

- Melody MIDI
- Bass MIDI
- Piano MIDI
- Synth MIDI
- Pluck MIDI
- Other melodic MIDI

MIDI must be musically accurate, cleanly organized, correctly named, and free of unnecessary events.

MIDI becomes **Asset 03 — MIDI Loop Kit**.

---

## 10. Stage 05 — Full Composition

The melody and stems are developed into the finished composition.

```text
16-BAR MELODY
      +
STEMS / ARRANGEMENT
      ↓
FULL COMPOSITION
      ↓
MIX
      ↓
MASTER
```

The composition becomes **Asset 04 — Beat Pack**.

---

## 11. Stage 06 — Mastering & Engineering Preservation

Final products receive a dedicated polish/mastering stage.

Never:

```text
Master → Export → Forget the chain
```

Instead:

```text
Master
  ├── Final Audio
  ├── Master Chain
  ├── Plugin Settings
  ├── Presets
  └── Relevant Engineering Notes
```

Potential chain components include EQ, compression, saturation, clipping, limiting, metering, and other corrective or tonal processing.

The exact chains used to polish the final commercial assets are retained as reusable technical assets and become part of the Sound Kit where licensing and product scope permit.

## 19. Quality-Control Gates

### Gate 01 — Sound QC
Audio quality; editing; gain; tuning; naming; metadata; preset pairing.

### Gate 02 — Melody QC
Musical quality; identity; key/BPM; 16-bar structure; product potential.

### Gate 03 — Stem QC
All required stems; correct names; lengths; alignment; source relationship.

### Gate 04 — MIDI QC
Musical accuracy; correct notes; timing; clean events; naming.

### Gate 05 — Composition QC
Arrangement; mix; dynamics; tonal balance; final production quality.

### Gate 06 — Master QC
Final loudness; true peak/headroom; clipping; translation; metadata; export integrity.

### Gate 07 — Product QC
File completeness; naming; folder structure; metadata; artwork; previews; licensing documentation.

### Gate 08 — Suite QC
Assets 01–04 complete; no broken references; no missing files; consistent versioning; premium bundle complete.

## 21. Separate Future Systems

The Core Production Asset Pipeline intentionally excludes drum one-shots, drum loops, drum kits, drum patterns, dedicated 808/drum workflows, FX libraries, FX loops, specialized transition design, and large-scale experimental sound-design systems.

These become separate modular systems:

```text
CORE MELODIC PIPELINE
        │
        ├── DRUM SYSTEM
        ├── FX SYSTEM
        └── SPECIALIZED SOUND DESIGN SYSTEM
```

They may eventually feed the composition engine without being mixed into the core asset taxonomy.

## 23. Omega House Production Laws

1. **SOURCE OF TRUTH** — Every production has one authoritative source session.
2. **PRESERVE LINEAGE** — Every derivative remains connected to its source.
3. **NEVER RECREATE UNNECESSARILY** — Derive downstream assets from upstream assets whenever possible.
4. **PRESERVE ENGINEERING** — Presets and master chains are assets, not disposable settings.
5. **SEPARATE ASSET CLASSES** — Melodic production, drums, FX, and specialized sound design remain modular.
6. **PRODUCTIZE THE WORK** — Every production is evaluated for every legitimate derivative.
7. **BUNDLE INSTEAD OF DUPLICATE** — The Production Suite aggregates existing assets.
8. **QC BEFORE PUBLICATION** — An asset is complete only when its technical and commercial requirements are satisfied.
